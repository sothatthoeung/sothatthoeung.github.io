About Me

I work in IT operations: Microsoft 365 and Entra ID, Ubuntu servers, Docker, Proxmox, and the DNS and firewall layer that connects them. I like problems that start as “it’s broken” and end as a documented fix that stops them coming back.

That habit is why I publish runbooks and root-cause write-ups on GitHub. Good operations is repeatable, and repeatable means written down.

My long-term goal is to lead technology as an IT Director, and later a CTO or CIO. I’m building the foundation in public: this page shows what I can already do, what I’m learning now, and what comes next. I only mark a skill as proficient when I can show the work.
